# SOL KYST Meta ad style (learned from Meghan's edits)

Canva doc: https://www.canva.com/d/15jxs2HYl3w7He_ ("SOL KYST Meta Ads: Editorial")

## What the edits taught us
- **Get close.** Tight, face-filling crops where the frames are the hero. Avoid wide lifestyle shots where the glasses are small.
- **Spec callouts.** Thin 1px ivory lines from the frame to small all-caps labels: POLARIZED, ACETATE, and the fit line with the real measurements (e.g. 63-17-145).
- **Moody, cinematic photos.** Car-window and shadow shots beat bright flash shots.
- **The fit promise goes in the eyebrow** in plain words, e.g. "Designed to fit & flatter wide face babes."
- **Headline:** big italic serif, 1–2 lines, centred low on the frame.
- **Footer:** small all-caps; benefit on the left, SOLKYST.CA on the right.

## Layout system
1080×1350 · full-bleed photo · soft cocoa gradient on the bottom half · eyebrow 22px · headline 140px italic serif · footer 22px.

## Rules
- No VIP-sale messaging (scrapped).
- Only claims we can source: sizes 135/145/158, free 45-day exchanges, free shipping over $150, duty-free CA/US over $150, ships from Hamilton, KIN #1 / Sweet Heart #2 (net sales).
- Real reviews only. Never write a quote.
