## Who we are
- Person-first: "we don't want you thinking or worrying about your sunglasses." When they fit right, look amazing and you feel confident, you can be your best self.
- "We put the world into shadows so you can shine."
- Redefining beauty standards with a global community of "wide face babes (and not so wide face babes)".
- Sunnies as "our armour and shield to take on the day". "A bomb pair of sunnies gives you an instant confidence boost + makes you feel like a bad B."

## Timeline
- **July 2020, the idea.** Lockdown, work shut down, living in downtown Toronto, "eating through my savings". On the beach in a cute cover-up and bathing suit, wearing "a stupid pair of sunglasses that made me look like John Lennon and Harry Potter had a baby". Not abnormally small glasses: "I am a wide faced cheeky baby and glasses are made in one standard size that has never worked for me." The idea "washed up on shore": make the widest and largest women's sunglasses on the market.
- **Summer–Oct 2020, the Kyst Eye.** Days researching (how eyes work, lens materials, acetate vs plastic), nights on calls with overseas factories, R&D and engineers. Design first, colours later. Used 10+ years as a professional freelance makeup artist: a lifted silhouette that enhances bone structure. Construction paper and scissors in front of the mirror, using highlight and contour principles. Many iterations "and a terrible Canva diagram" later: first prototype. The Kyst Eye is still the same design today.
- **End of 2020.** First prototype back, teased on socials. Everyone asked for black, so she ordered a black pair.
- **March 2021, viral.** Showed the black prototype on TikTok: 120,000 views in the first few days. No Shopify store yet; a landing page got 1,200 email sign-ups over the weekend. Built the website in 3 days, opened with one product (black, one giant size) and had a **$10,000+ launch day**. "Exciting, amazing but really freaking incredibly scary" because inventory was three months away.
  - NOT "a pre-order video brought in $10,000 in 24 hours". That wording is wrong.
- **June 2021, launch.** Full colour collection, ~350 orders shipped. Within two weeks, 50+ five-star reviews, "some essay length long".

## Today
- Customers in 50+ countries. 650+ five-star reviews.
- Three sizes: Mini, Midi, Wide (135 / 145 / 158mm). "The first and only size inclusive eyewear brand" (Meghan's claim).
- Featured in fashion publications. Aired on CBC's Dragons' Den (two Dragons wanted to partner; don't name them). Barbie collab: full 8-piece licensed collection. Recently aired on QVC.

## Concept
- Independent eyewear is expensive with huge minimums; three sizes per style triples inventory. So new styles are strategic: neutral tones that work for everyone, classic silhouettes. "Intentional, versatile and beautifully crafted for fashion and function."

## Materials
- The first thing people notice is the weight: substantial, no traditional plastic.
- Acetate frames, UV400, nylon polarized lenses, extra anti-scratch coating. Luxury packaging.
