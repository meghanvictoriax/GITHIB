# Meghan's voice

Use for anything signed by Meghan or written in first person (founder emails, plain-text notes, captions).
Source: Meghan's own writing (Oct 1, 5, 6 and 13), picked out in a ChatGPT review. Only her lines are quoted below.

## In one line
Talking honestly to someone she trusts: a specific moment, how it felt, what she learned. Warm, self-aware, a bit sweary, funny in passing. Her best lines put something blunt next to something tender.

## Her lines (reference, not copy)
- "There's really not many people I don't get along with, and I definitely prefer to be liked by people, that's for sure."
- "And then in the end it ends up biting me in the ass … and as I like to say my heart hurts."
- "I think the biggest hauntings I have in this business are when I've said yes to things that I haven't felt good about."
- "…spraying a signature scent on the bed because somehow that immediately tells my brain, okay, we're home for now."
- "Like, go back to the '90s with the ugly-ass calendar. Like, I remember when the Sears catalog would come out…"
- "Unfortunately when you're running a small company and a small team, there's a lot of hats you have to wear."
- "I just want to say thank you so much again for such a freaking phenomenal and fun day."

## Do
- Write the way she talks. Contractions, "honestly", "like", "that's for sure", "freaking". Sentences can run on a little.
- Start from a real, specific scene (a product, a place, a moment), then say how it felt.
- Admit something a bit uncomfortable about herself, then what she learned from it.
- Pair blunt with tender: "biting me in the ass" + "my heart hurts".
- Explain ideas through shared memories (the Sears catalog).
- Thank people like you mean it, out loud.
- Light swearing is on brand ("ass", "freaking"). Keep anything stronger out of customer email.

## Don't
- No polished marketing voice: "elevate", "curated", "game-changer", "we're thrilled to announce".
- No neat three-part slogans or "It's not X. It's Y." lines.
- No invented anecdotes. Specific scenes have to be real. If a draft needs one, ask Meghan.
- Her personal stories (hospital, travel) stay out of marketing unless she says so.
- Trim filler "likes" in edited copy, but keep the momentum and the run-on rhythm.
