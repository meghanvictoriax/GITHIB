Hi, it's Meghan, the founder of SOL KYST. Let me take you back to summer 2020.

It's lockdown. Work is shut down, I'm living in downtown Toronto, eating through my savings and wondering what the heck I'm going to do. One day I'm sitting on the beach in my cute cover-up and my bathing suit… and a stupid pair of sunglasses that made me look like John Lennon and Harry Potter had a baby.

I wasn't feeling cute. I wasn't feeling confident. And it was all because of my stupidly small glasses. They weren't even abnormally small. I'm just a wide-faced cheeky babe, and sunglasses come in one standard size that has never worked for me.

And right there, the idea washed up on shore: I'm going to make the widest, largest women's sunglasses on the market.

I'd been a makeup artist for 10+ years, so I sat in front of my mirror with construction paper and scissors and used highlight and contour to cut out a shape that actually flatters a wider face. Many, many paper cut-outs later (and a terrible Canva diagram), that became our Kyst Eye. Same design we use today.

In March 2021 I showed the first black prototype on TikTok, not expecting anything. 120,000 views in a few days and 1,200 sign-ups over one weekend. I threw a website together in three days and had a $10,000+ launch day. Exciting, amazing and really freaking scary, because the inventory was three months away.

Now we're in three sizes (Mini 135, Midi 145 and Wide 158mm), with 650+ five-star reviews and customers in over 50 countries. And on CBC's Dragons' Den, two Dragons wanted to partner with us.

Because let's be real: a bomb pair of sunnies is your armour for the day. You deserve one that fits.

Love, Meghan
Founder, SOL KYST
