Hi, it's Meghan. I'm the founder of SOL KYST, and I want to tell you why this brand exists, because honestly, it's a little personal.

I've been a makeup artist for years, so I spend a lot of time looking at faces. And for just as long, I couldn't find a single pair of sunglasses that fit mine. I have a wide face. Every pair pinched my temples, left red marks by lunch and looked a size too small. I genuinely thought something was wrong with my face.

Turns out it was the frames. Almost everything out there comes in one size, about 140mm, and if that's not you, well, good luck.

So I designed the pair I couldn't find, in three widths: Mini 135, Midi 145 and Wide 158mm. I posted a pre-order video and it brought in $10,000 in 24 hours. My heart was so full. That's when I knew it wasn't just me.

Then we walked into CBC's Dragons' Den, and two Dragons wanted to partner with us. I still kind of can't believe I get to type that sentence.

So if you've ever tried on pair after pair and walked out with nothing, this one's for you. Your face was never the problem.

Love, Meghan
